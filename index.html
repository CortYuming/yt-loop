What to bring in

Videos

Playing a range records it here, and editing Start / End / speed / Note while it plays rewrites that same entry — so what you settle on is what's kept. Replaying a range at the same speed moves its entry back to the top instead of adding another. Each video keeps its 5 most recent ranges; videos themselves stay until you clear them with the 🗑 in their header, which also drops that video's sheet. Bookmark a 🔗 URL to keep a range for good. All of it lives in your browser's localStorage.
